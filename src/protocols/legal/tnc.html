‍5. Breaks, Buffer & SchedulingMembers may take planned breaks during their protocol within the buffer allowance for their plan. The buffer scales with protocol duration and inclusions, and the specific allowance applicable to you is set out in your Consent Form and Protocol Confirmation. Breaks must be communicated to the care team in advance to activate the buffer period; remaining sessions are rescheduled based on mutual availability.

Extended breaks beyond the standard allowance may be granted in case of medical emergencies or other unavoidable circumstances, subject to Praan Health’s review on a case-by-case basis. If the buffer allowance is exhausted, remaining unused sessions will be considered lapsed and the protocol will be marked concluded.

Where any member of your care team is unavailable due to leave, illness, reassignment, or any other operational reason, the affected sessions will not count against your break allowance. Disruptions caused by Praan Health’s internal operations are covered by the company, and those sessions will be adjusted and appended to the end of the protocol at no additional cost.

‍7. Protocol Conclusion & Close-Out
‍A protocol is considered concluded when the Member completes their enrolled protocol (of the duration set out in their Protocol Confirmation) and chooses not to renew — specifically, when all the included sessions, calls, and consultations have been completed. Conclusion is not simply the passing of time; it is a structured close-out designed to ensure you graduate with a documented record of progress and forward recommendations.

Where applicable to your protocol, the close-out may include the following steps, completed in order. Steps tied to components not included in your protocol do not apply:
a. Blood Panel Booking (where your protocol includes clinical review): the care team coordinates a blood work test to benchmark progress against your baseline.
b. Final Assessment (where your protocol includes training or functional testing): the relevant care team member re-administers the baseline assessment to measure progress.
c. Final Praan Doctor Consultation (where your protocol includes doctor consultations): a concluding consultation reviews your results, progress, and forward recommendations.
d. Final Progress Report & Recommendations: you receive a written summary covering baseline vs. end-of-protocol metrics for the components included in your plan, progress observed, and a recommendations document for maintenance and follow-ups.
‍
‍Where your protocol requires a closing blood panel or other report, Members must submit it within the timeframe set out in their Consent Form. Failure to submit within this period will result in the protocol being marked concluded without a final clinical review, after which no further feedback or protocol-related support will be provided. Once the close-out is complete — or if you do not respond or submit required reports within the stipulated period — the protocol is formally marked concluded, care team bandwidth is released, and access to protocol support ceases. Any future engagement requires fresh enrolment.
